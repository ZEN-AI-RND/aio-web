Screen guide

The dashboard sits on the left, Tanya ZARA on the right (on a phone the chat covers the screen). The written answer is always in the chat; the dashboard changes in the highlighted places.

How to try it (3 steps)

  1. Open skala-aioinsight.aioffice.com.my in Chrome, Edge or Safari, and tap the Zara button (bottom right).
  2. Paste a prompt from the prompt list, or tap 🎤 and say it. End with “hantar” or “send”.
  3. Watch the page: filters apply, the right chart zooms, or a new chart appears under Daripada Tanya ZARA.
